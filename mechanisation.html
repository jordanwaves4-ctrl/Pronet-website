mechanisation & piped systems

Upgrading hand pumps into solar-powered systems and running piped water to standpipes.

A mechanised system turns a queue at a pump into taps in a courtyard. We size the tank and pump to the dry-season demand, not the wet-season average, and train two caretakers per site.
