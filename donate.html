Institutional partners

If your organisation funds WASH, we would rather start with evidence than a pitch. Send the brief below and we will reply with a costed assessment of the communities that fit it — before you commit anything.

We build directly with our own technical team. Where a partner requires its own contractor, we will supervise the works and hand over the monitoring record.

What a partner gets before committing

A costed assessment. Household survey, water-point map and yield and quality tests for the community in question — before any construction is quoted.

A line-item budget. Water, sanitation, hygiene and training costed separately, with the maintenance years included rather than assumed.

A capacity record. Our registration, audited accounts, three years of project files and the functionality figures for every site we have handed over.
